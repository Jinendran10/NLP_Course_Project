Senior Data Engineer - Acme Payments

About the role
We are looking for a data engineer to build and scale the pipelines behind our fraud and risk
products. You will own ingestion, modelling and data quality for payment events.

Requirements:
• 4+ years of experience with Python and SQL
• Hands-on experience with Apache Spark, Kafka and Airflow
• Experience with a cloud data warehouse such as Snowflake or BigQuery
• Strong knowledge of AWS and infrastructure as code (Terraform)
• Comfortable with Docker and Kubernetes

Nice to have:
• Experience with dbt and Great Expectations
• Familiarity with machine learning workflows and MLflow
• Exposure to Java or Scala
• Excellent communication skills
